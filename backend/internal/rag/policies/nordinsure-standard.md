# NordInsure Standard Replacement Cover (NI-STD) - fictional demo contract

## Eligibility
NordInsure covers a replacement vehicle when the insured car is off the road because of an accident or a covered breakdown and is being repaired at a partner garage. Cosmetic repairs that leave the car drivable are not covered.

## Vehicle category
The replacement vehicle must be of the same category as the insured car, or the next category up when the same category is not available. The maximum category NordInsure pays for is COMPACT. A downgrade below the insured car's category is never allowed.

## Rental duration
NordInsure pays for the estimated repair time, up to a maximum of 10 days per claim. If the garage estimate is longer than 10 days, book the maximum of 10 days and raise an extension request; extensions need written approval from the NordInsure claims handler.

## Customer co-payment
If the customer wants a vehicle above the covered category (for example an SUV), the customer pays the daily price difference directly. The customer must explicitly agree to the co-payment before the booking is confirmed. Never upgrade at NordInsure's cost.

## Delivery and collection
Delivery to the customer's home or to the garage is not covered by NordInsure. The customer collects the vehicle at a branch in the same city.

## Exclusions
One-way rentals, cross-border trips and additional drivers are not covered by NordInsure Standard.
