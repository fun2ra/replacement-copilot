# FleetLease Premium Mobility Guarantee (FL-PREM) - fictional demo contract

## Eligibility
FleetLease Premium covers a replacement vehicle for any leased vehicle that is off the road for maintenance, accident repair or breakdown, from the first day.

## Vehicle category
The replacement vehicle should match the leased vehicle's category. If no like-for-like vehicle is available at the nearest branch, FleetLease accepts one category up, with a maximum category of SUV. PREMIUM vehicles are never covered. A downgrade is not allowed.

## Rental duration
FleetLease pays for the full repair time up to 21 days. Longer repairs require a new authorisation from the FleetLease fleet manager.

## Transmission and equipment
If the driver profile states automatic only, the replacement must be automatic. Child seats and other equipment noted on the case are booked as extras at no cost to the driver.

## Delivery and collection
FleetLease covers delivery of the replacement vehicle to the garage or to the driver's address within the same city.

## Exclusions
Cross-border trips need prior approval from the fleet manager.
